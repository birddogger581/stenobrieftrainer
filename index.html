How to Use Brief Builder

Step 1: Input List
Paste your words or phrases you would like to train on into the box. Separate each item by pressing Enter to place it on a brand new line. Maximum of 10 allowed.
Step 2: Ensure Translation Software is running
Make sure you have your translation software (casecat, plover etc.) open and ready to translate your steno strokes.
Step 3: Press Start Training
Press Start Training to load your words and phrases!
Step 4: Listen and Stroke
Listen to the words or phrases and type them into the box.
Step 5 Real-time Feedback:
Answers will be graded with green letters meaning your entry is correct. Red strike-through tags highlight typos, while faded yellow elements indicate missing characters.

Correct answers will change the background color based on time. Green is very fast, Blue is good, Yellow is a little long and Red is too long.
