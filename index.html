How to Use Brief Builder

Step 1: Input List
Paste your words on new lines. Separate items with Enter. Max 5 allowed.
Step 2: Translation Software
Ensure software like Plover or CaseCat is running and translating strokes.
Step 3: Start Training
Press Start Training to load your phrases!
Step 4: Stroke and Listen
Listen to the prompts and type them into the text box.
Step 5: Feedback
Background colors will change based on how fast you answer! Results will come when the timer ends.
